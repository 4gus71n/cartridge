## Voice clips — LibriVox (public domain)
Every voice clip in `content/audio/` is a short excerpt (5–8 s) of a
[LibriVox](https://librivox.org) audiobook recording, downloaded from the
LibriVox collection on [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/librivoxaudio).
**All LibriVox recordings are dedicated to the public domain** (see
<https://librivox.org/pages/public-domain/>); each archive.org item used here
carries the Public Domain Mark / CC0 licence URL listed in the table below and
in `content/clips.json`. The underlying texts are public-domain works.

Processing (by `tools/curate.py`): an HTTP byte range is fetched from the middle
of one chapter (never the first 60 s, which hold the spoken LibriVox
disclaimer), the most speech-dense 5.5–7 s window is selected with ffmpeg
`silencedetect`, loudness-normalised to −18 LUFS (EBU R128, two-pass), faded
in/out, and encoded as mono Ogg Vorbis (~40 kbit/s). Windows with too few
speech pauses (music / noise beds) or too little speech are rejected.

Thank you to every LibriVox volunteer reader listed below.
